# About Me

I'm Masood Ur Rehman, a Lead Software Engineer, AI Developer, and founder of **CWMStudio**.

My journey started with full-stack development, building applications and solving real-world problems through software. Over time, it evolved into freelancing, working with businesses, and eventually building my own independent software studio.

Today, my focus is on creating independent software products, developing custom digital solutions, and exploring the possibilities of AI and agentic development.

Through CWMStudio, I bring ideas to life, build practical products, and help businesses move forward with modern technology.

Alongside development, I share technical knowledge through YouTube tutorials and articles, documenting what I learn and building in public.

I believe in clean engineering, meaningful products, creative freedom, and continuous growth.
